◆ close_db_connections()

app.app.close_db_connections (   _exc = None)

Teardown handler to close (or return) DB connections after each request.

Connections are tracked on Flask's g object as g._db_conns and will be closed at the end of the request, returning pooled connections to the pool if enabled.
